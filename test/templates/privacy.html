🌾 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Dutipay collects information to provide attendance and payroll services:

  • Business information (name, logo, contact details)
  • Employee information (name, position, salary, schedule)
  • Attendance records (time-in, time-out, photos)
  • Payroll data (days worked, salary computation)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide attendance tracking and payroll services
  • Generate reports for your business
  • Improve our system and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage, role-based access, and per-business data isolation.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Your business data is isolated and only accessible by authorized users within your organization.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at support@Dutipay.app for any data-related requests.

6. Data Retention

Attendance records, including photos, are retained for a maximum of 12 months. Payroll records are retained for 5 years as required by Philippine tax and labor regulations. You may request early deletion of your data by contacting support.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@Dutipay.app.
